Mobile app data paths

AI and data use

Routine organization is local to your device. When an optional app feature uses a network, its request follows a separate path described below.

This page describes the mobile app. Website handling is separate and is covered in the privacy overview.

What stays on your device

The app stores your routine, product shelf, preferences, Routine Snapshot answers, and check-ins on your device by default. A DermaPrivate account is not required. AI keys are stored in secure device storage.

Optional AI Assist: device to Groq

  1. You choose to enable AI Assist and review the information request in the app.
  2. The app narrows the request to information relevant to that AI action and checks authorization before sending.
  3. For the current iOS release settings, the app sends an HTTPS request directly from the device to Groq’s API at api.groq.com/openai/v1/chat/completions. The request includes your key as the Groq authorization credential.

The BYOK request does not pass through a DermaPrivate routine server. Groq receives the approved request and its API credential; Groq’s own terms and data handling apply. AI is optional and does not silently replace your saved routine.

Groq says it retains usage metadata and does not normally retain inference inputs or outputs. It may temporarily log inputs and outputs for service reliability or abuse investigation for up to 30 days; Groq’s data controls describe an organization-level opt-out. DermaPrivate cannot see which Groq data-control setting applies to your account. Read Groq’s current data-handling details ↗ before sending information.

Online product lookup

The app checks available catalogs and its on-device lookup cache first. A product-name or routine-step/category search, or a lookup by barcode number, can then go directly from your device to Open Beauty Facts. The request contains the search text or barcode and the technical fields needed for the lookup; it does not include your profile, Snapshot answers, ingredient avoidances, or routine. A result may be cached on your device.

Open Beauty Facts receives the network request, including an IP address and ordinary request metadata. Its published visitor policy covers its services and describes IP/log retention, but does not separately specify whether each direct API request path, search term, or barcode is retained. The exact production API logging for those fields is not verified. Read the published policy and the limits of what is known about API logs.

External product links

When you choose to open a product link, this release opens a direct brand website or a Google product search. It does not configure Rakuten or another affiliate network, add affiliate campaign identifiers, or earn commission or purchase attribution through these links. The destination receives the URL, which may include a product or brand name, and ordinary connection information; its privacy practices apply after you leave the app.

Website data is separate

This page concerns the mobile app. The website does not collect an app key or app routine. For the website’s static files, host requests, and the limits of what has been confirmed about hosting logs, see the privacy overview.
