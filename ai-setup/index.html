Optional AI Assist

AI setup

AI is optional. Your Routine Snapshot and core routine tools work locally without it.

i

Your choice, your key. DermaPrivate does not use a shared AI key. If you choose AI Assist, create a Groq API key and add it in the app. Your key stays securely stored on your device.

Setup steps

  1. Open the Groq API keys console ↗.
  2. Sign in or create a Groq account, then create a new API key. Groq's account and provider terms apply.
  3. Copy the key once and keep it in a secure password manager.
  4. Open DermaPrivate and add the key on the AI setup screen or in AI settings.
  5. Review the data-use information and consent before AI requests are sent.

What happens when you use AI

Approved information goes directly from your device to Groq. DermaPrivate does not route AI requests through a DermaPrivate routine server. The app's local Routine Snapshot remains the source of truth, and AI does not change your saved routine automatically.

Optional AI Assist can provide cosmetic skincare education and explain a routine starting point. It does not diagnose skin conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical advice.
