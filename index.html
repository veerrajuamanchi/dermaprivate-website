Product lookup checks available local records and the 30-day cache first. If it needs an online match, a product-name or routine-step/category search or a lookup by barcode number goes directly from your device to Open Beauty Facts. It does not send your profile or routine. Read what Open Beauty Facts publishes about IP and logs, and what it does not separately specify for API requests.

AI Assist is optional and, in this release, requires your own Groq account and API key. Before you authorize a request, the app shows the specific information to be sent.
