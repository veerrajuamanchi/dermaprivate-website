Ingredient information may be incomplete or may change after a product reformulation. Refer to current product packaging and manufacturer information before use.

Open Beauty Facts

The DermaPrivate app source contains a product lookup fallback for information not found in available catalogs or local cache. Open Beauty Facts is part of the Open Food Facts ecosystem. The source code constructs an “Open Beauty Facts (ODbL)” label for results from that lookup path, but we have not confirmed that this path or label is exposed in the current release. This website page does not replace the source and license information that should accompany third-party content in the app.

Open Beauty Facts distinguishes between:

  • The database, made available under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • Individual database contents, which may be covered by the Database Contents License (DbCL).
  • Product images, which Open Beauty Facts describes as available under a Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike license, subject to any applicable source terms and additional rights in the image.

The applicable image license version, record-level source information, attribution details, modifications, and any separate rights must be checked for the material actually used. A general source label does not establish permission for an individual image. This website does not state that all displayed Open Beauty Facts-derived material has been cleared.

Relevant source and license information:

Rights questions and corrections
