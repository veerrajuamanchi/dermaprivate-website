Privacy & data

DermaPrivate Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Privacy contact: support@amanchilabs.com

1. Who we are

DermaPrivate is operated by Amanchi Labs LLC, doing business as Amanchi Labs (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the DermaPrivate mobile app or visit our website at www.dermaprivate.app. The app and website have different data practices, described below.

DermaPrivate is a local-first cosmetic skincare routine organization app. Its core routine features do not require a DermaPrivate account or a continuous internet connection. Optional online features communicate with the services described in this policy.

DermaPrivate is not a medical service. It does not diagnose skin conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace instructions from a qualified healthcare professional.

2. Privacy at a glance

Your routines, saved products, profile preferences, and check-ins are stored on your device by default.

We do not provide account-based routine synchronization or operate a cloud backup service for your routines.

Product lookup and recommendations check available local records and the 30-day on-device lookup cache before sending a product name, routine-step category, or barcode number to Open Beauty Facts for an online match.

Optional AI Assist uses your own Groq API key and sends selected information to Groq after you authorize the request.

Product lookup, AI requests, purchases, support email, and user-directed exports or external links involve separate services, as described below.

Our website provides information about the app and does not process purchases.

You can use the app’s settings to manage local data and optional AI features.

3. Information stored in the mobile app

Depending on the features you use, DermaPrivate stores the following information on your device:

  • Skincare preferences, such as skin type, sensitivity, goals, ingredient avoidances, and routine preferences.
  • Products you add, including product names and available ingredient information.
  • Routine steps, morning and evening schedules, and related settings.
  • Routine Snapshot answers and starting-point suggestions.
  • Check-ins and routine history, including steps you mark complete.
  • AI consent choices and a local AI activity history.
  • Your Groq API key, if you choose to configure AI Assist.

Routine and profile information is stored locally. The Groq API key is stored using the device’s secure credential storage.

The local AI activity history records request metadata, such as the feature used, provider, request status, data categories, timing, and token counts. It is designed not to store raw AI prompts or responses and is limited to the 100 most recent entries. Suggestions you choose to save may become part of your locally stored routine information.

Device backups

Local information may be included in device backups according to your operating-system and backup settings. Those backups are separate from DermaPrivate; we do not operate a routine backup or synchronization service.

Settings also lets you create an optional encrypted backup file containing your routine snapshot and schedule. You choose a passphrase; the Groq API key is not included. The app creates the file locally and opens the device’s system sharing interface so you can save or send it to a destination you select. DermaPrivate does not receive the file. The destination may retain it under its own practices, and the file can be read by someone who has both the file and passphrase.

Advanced Skin Insights can be saved as a PDF. The report includes wellbeing scores and interpretation, beard-friction scoring and guidance, Fitzpatrick phototype and related risk/protection guidance, and the completion date. Choosing Save PDF opens the device’s system sharing interface; the report is not automatically sent to DermaPrivate. The destination you select controls any further handling or retention.

Local-first storage does not mean that every app record is separately encrypted by DermaPrivate. Device security, passcode protection, and backup settings also affect protection of your information.

4. App network requests

Online features may disclose ordinary connection information, including your IP address, request time, and technical request headers, to the receiving service and its infrastructure.

Open Beauty Facts product lookup

The app checks available local catalogs and its on-device lookup cache before using Open Beauty Facts. Successful lookup responses are cached locally for 30 days. Identical lookups made at the same time share one request; the app applies a local limit of 8 requests per 60 seconds and stops waiting after 6 seconds.

If there is no local match, a product-name or routine-step/category search sends only the search text to Open Beauty Facts’ search endpoint. A lookup by barcode number sends the barcode to its product endpoint.

These requests go directly from your device to Open Beauty Facts. They include an identifying User-Agent containing the DermaPrivate app name, version, and business support email address. They do not include your skin profile, Snapshot answers, ingredient avoidances, pregnancy/nursing context, or routine details.

The request includes an IP address and technical request metadata needed to return the response. Open Beauty Facts' published privacy policy covers its services and lists visitor IP addresses and logs with a three-year storage period for security, technical analyses, and popularity measurement. It does not separately specify whether direct API request paths, search text, or barcode values are included in those logs. Public server configuration can log a request URI with IP address, timestamp, and User-Agent, but that does not verify the exact configuration or retention applied to each live API request. Treat information Open Beauty Facts processes under its policy; contact the provider for details about specific API fields.

Product listings may have missing, incomplete, or outdated ingredient fields. Missing ingredients are treated as unknown. A product match or absence of a listed concern does not establish suitability. You can add an unmatched product manually and enter ingredient text; that text is user-provided and is not independently verified. Product information you deliberately save to your routine may remain after the lookup cache expires.

Amanchi Labs does not receive these searches or barcodes through this lookup process.

5. Optional AI Assist and Groq

AI Assist is optional. It is not required for core routine setup, editing, daily check-ins, or on-device compatibility rules.

To use AI Assist, you configure your own Groq API key. Authorized requests are sent directly from your device to Groq, with the key supplied as an authentication credential.

Information sent

Depending on the feature, an AI request may include:

  • Product names, types, brands, or regions.
  • Routine windows and step labels.
  • Confirmed ingredient lists or active ingredient families.
  • Ingredient avoidances.
  • Skin type, sensitivity, and cosmetic goals.
  • Local rule findings.
  • Routine Snapshot preferences, results, summaries, or related notes.
  • Prior AI suggestions relevant to the requested feature.

The in-app consent flow identifies the information categories used for the request. Review that disclosure before authorizing AI use.

The app does not intentionally include face photographs, email addresses, account identifiers, or free-form journal entries in AI prompts. Product names and other user-entered fields may nevertheless contain information you choose to enter; avoid including unnecessary personal information.

Groq’s data handling

Groq processes requests under its own terms, privacy policy, and account settings.

Groq states that it collects usage metadata and does not retain inference inputs and outputs by default. It may retain inputs and outputs in limited circumstances, including reliability troubleshooting or abuse investigation. Its documentation describes retention of up to 30 days for those logs, unless longer retention is legally required, and offers Zero Data Retention controls for eligible requests.

Your Groq organization’s settings affect its handling of requests. DermaPrivate cannot view or change those settings.

For current information, see:

  • Groq data controls
  • Groq privacy policy

Your AI controls

You can revoke AI consent, remove your stored API key, and clear local AI activity history in Settings.

These actions do not delete information already processed by Groq. Removing a key from DermaPrivate does not revoke it in your Groq account. To revoke the credential or make a request about information Groq holds, use Groq’s account controls or privacy channels.

6. Purchases and external shopping links

App store purchases

If you purchase Pro Offline or restore a purchase, the applicable store—Apple App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android—processes payment and provides purchase or entitlement information needed to enable the relevant features.

DermaPrivate does not receive your payment-card number. Apple or Google maintains its transaction records under its own terms and privacy policy.

The app stores a local entitlement record on this device to support access and ownership recovery. Clearing routine or profile information does not cancel a store purchase or delete the store’s transaction records. You can use the app’s restore-purchase function with the relevant Apple ID or Google Play account.

Brand and retailer links

When you choose to open a product link, the app opens a direct brand website or a Google product search. The destination receives the URL, which may include the product or brand name, and ordinary connection information. Its privacy practices apply after you follow the link.

This release does not configure affiliate links or send affiliate campaign identifiers. DermaPrivate does not receive notice that you opened a product link or made a purchase through it.

The app does not intentionally include your skin profile or routine information in shopping URLs.

A product link is not a guarantee of suitability. Product information, prices, and availability may be incomplete or change.

7. The DermaPrivate website

The website provides information about the mobile app. It does not require an account or offer checkout.

When you request a page or website asset, our hosting, delivery, or security providers may receive technical information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Requested page or file.
  • Referring page, where supplied.
  • Request date and time.

This information may be used to deliver the website, maintain security, and diagnose service problems.

Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

8. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you provide.

We use this information to respond, provide support, and maintain necessary business records. Our email service providers also process messages under their own terms and privacy practices.

Please do not send API keys, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information.

9. How we use and disclose information

We use information to:

  • Provide the website and app features you request.
  • Maintain locally stored routines and preferences.
  • Support optional product lookup.
  • Obtain an AI response when you authorize a request.
  • Enable or restore purchases.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Maintain security and troubleshoot services.

Information is disclosed to recipients as described in this policy. We may also disclose information we hold when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, prevent fraud, or protect rights and safety.

The app does not provide an account-based routine server or routine synchronization service. Locally stored routines are not uploaded to us merely because you create or use them.

10. Retention and deletion

Local app information

Routine and profile information remains on your device until you remove it or clear the relevant app data, subject to device and backup behavior.

Settings provides controls to clear local routine/profile information, clear AI activity history, remove your API key, and revoke AI consent. These controls have different scopes; clearing routine information does not necessarily clear credentials, AI activity, or purchase-entitlement information.

Removing the app may not remove information retained in device backups or secure credential storage.

Information held by us

We retain support correspondence and operational records for as long as reasonably necessary for their stated purposes, including responding to requests, security, troubleshooting, and legal obligations.

Technical-log retention depends on the service and its configuration. Contact us with questions about information we control.

Information held by other organizations

Deleting local data does not delete information already processed by Apple, Google Play, Groq, Open Beauty Facts, email providers, or a product-link destination. Their retention practices and applicable privacy rights continue to apply.

Where a provider processes information on our behalf, we will handle applicable requests concerning information under our control.

11. Your choices and privacy requests

You can:

  • Use core routine features without enabling AI Assist.
  • Decline or revoke AI authorization.
  • Remove your Groq API key.
  • Clear local AI activity history.
  • Delete local routine/profile information.
  • Choose whether to open external shopping links.
  • Manage device backup settings.

To ask a privacy question or exercise rights concerning information we control, contact support@amanchilabs.com.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. We may need to verify a request before acting. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Because DermaPrivate does not provide an account or routine-sync service, we generally cannot retrieve or remotely erase information stored only on your device.

12. Security

We use device storage, secure credential storage, and HTTPS network connections for their intended purposes. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Protect your device with a passcode, keep its operating system updated, and review your backup settings. External providers maintain their own security controls.

13. International processing

Information sent to service providers may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Applicable protections and transfer practices depend on the provider and the laws that apply.

For example, Groq’s documentation states that retained customer data is stored in the United States. Refer to provider privacy notices for current information.

14. Children’s privacy

DermaPrivate is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our website or support channels.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support@amanchilabs.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. We will publish the current version on the DermaPrivate website and update the dates above.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before introducing materially different processing.
