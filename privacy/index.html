Local-first, in plain language

DermaPrivate is designed to keep routine planning close to you. Core local functionality is intended to work without requiring an account, subject to the released app’s actual behavior. Review the final policy for the precise data, storage, backup, and account details.

Optional AI Assist

When available, Optional AI Assist is a beta feature. Before an AI request, DermaPrivate asks you to review and consent to the approved, minimized information being sent to your selected AI provider. Provider and infrastructure terms may apply. AI explanations are cosmetic education only and do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or change your routine automatically.

Website privacy

This marketing website has no analytics or tracking scripts, advertising pixels, session replay, third-party chat widgets, or marketing SDKs.
