Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 14, 2026 · Version 1.0

DermaPrivate is a cosmetic skincare routine organizer by Amanchi Labs. This policy explains information handled by the DermaPrivate website and mobile app. The app and website have separate data flows, described below.

DermaPrivate is not a medical service. It does not diagnose skin conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace advice from a qualified health professional. Do not enter information you do not want stored on your device or included in an optional feature you choose to use.

At a glance

  • You do not need a DermaPrivate account to organize a routine.
  • Routine and profile information is stored on your device by default. DermaPrivate does not provide account-based routine synchronization in the current app.
  • Optional app features contact separate services. An authorized AI request goes directly from your device to Groq. A product search may send your search text to Open Beauty Facts. The app may request catalog updates and remote product images, and it opens brand or retailer links when you choose them.
  • Those services receive ordinary connection data, such as your IP address, when your device makes a network request. Each service has its own privacy practices and retention rules.
  • You can use core routine features without enabling AI or performing an online product search.

1. Who operates DermaPrivate

DermaPrivate is a product by Amanchi Labs. In this policy, “Amanchi Labs,” “DermaPrivate,” “we,” and “us” refer to the operator of the DermaPrivate website and mobile app. For privacy questions, contact support@amanchilabs.com.

2. DermaPrivate website

The website is made up of informational pages and a small script for navigation and FAQ controls. The website code does not add analytics, advertising pixels, or embedded third-party video. It does not require an account.

When you visit, the website host receives the requests needed to deliver pages and files. The host and any delivery or security providers may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested page or file, referring page, and request time. These providers may use request information to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website. Their logging and retention settings are controlled by them; we do not promise a specific log-retention period.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and provide support. Your email provider also processes the message under its own practices. The website may link to other sites; when you follow a link, the destination site handles your request under its own policy.

3. Information stored in the mobile app

The app stores information you enter or create on your device so its core features can work without a DermaPrivate account. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • skin preferences and goals, sensitivity selections, and Routine Snapshot answers;
  • products you add, product details and notes, ingredient information, routine steps, and AM/PM schedules;
  • assessments, Snapshot results, routine check-ins, and history;
  • app preferences, AI authorization choices, and AI activity records; and
  • a Groq API key, if you choose to add your own key for AI Assist.

The Groq key is stored in the device’s secure credential storage. Other routine and profile information is kept in the app’s local storage by default. The local AI activity history records request metadata, such as the feature, provider, status, data categories, timing, and token counts. It is designed not to store the prompt, response, product names, personal notes, or API key, and it keeps up to 100 recent entries.

Information stored on your device may be included in device backups according to your operating-system and backup settings. The app can create an encrypted export backup that you choose where to save; the Groq key is not included. Anyone with access to an exported backup and its passphrase may be able to access the backed-up information. DermaPrivate does not operate a routine backup or sync service and does not promise that all local app storage is separately encrypted by the app.

4. Optional AI Assist

AI Assist is optional. Routine setup, editing, check-ins, and the app’s local compatibility rules do not require AI. Before an AI request is sent, the app asks for authorization and shows the recipient and information categories for the feature. You can decline, revoke authorization in Settings, remove your key, or choose to be asked before each request. Saved authorization expires after 12 months and is limited to its approved provider, mode, and information categories. A missing, declined, expired, or revoked authorization blocks the request.

When you use AI Assist in the current app, the request is sent directly from your device to Groq over HTTPS using the API key you supplied. DermaPrivate does not receive that key or proxy the request through a DermaPrivate AI server. Depending on the AI feature, a request may contain selected routine details, product types or names, routine windows and placement, active ingredient families or confirmed ingredient names, local compatibility findings, skin goals or skin type/sensitivity, ingredient priorities or avoidances, Snapshot preferences and results, or prior AI suggestions. A connection test sends a fixed test prompt only when you tap the test control. The app’s AI consent screen lists the information categories for each feature. API keys and personal notes are not placed in AI prompts.

Groq’s data-handling documentation says it retains usage metadata. Groq says prompts and responses are not retained by default, but may be temporarily logged for reliability or abuse investigation for up to 30 days unless Zero Data Retention is enabled for the applicable organization. DermaPrivate cannot verify a user’s Groq organization setting. Groq’s privacy policy and terms apply to information it receives. Revoking authorization stops future requests; it does not delete information already sent to Groq.

Hosted AI and hosted AI-usage requests are disabled in the current app. If this changes, we will update this policy and the in-app disclosure before enabling the feature.

5. Product search, catalog updates, and images

The app checks its available local, bundled, cached, and downloaded catalog information first. If those sources do not provide a match and you choose an online product lookup, the app sends the product name or barcode you entered to Open Beauty Facts at world.openbeautyfacts.org. The request also contains the app’s static User-Agent value, DermaPrivate/1.0.1 (support@amanchilabs.com), and ordinary network information such as your IP address. The search does not include your routine or skin profile. The app limits this fallback to no more than eight requests in a 60-second window within a running app session. Open Beauty Facts’ own service information and practices apply to the request it receives.

The app may check api.dermaprivate.app for catalog updates. That request includes a rollout bucket from 0 to 99 and ordinary network information; it is not designed to send your routine, profile, or raw installation identifier. If an update is available, product catalog data may be downloaded and cached on your device. The catalog service may process request information to deliver and troubleshoot the service. Its server logs and retention are controlled by the hosting provider; no specific retention period is promised here.

When a remote product image is displayed, your device requests the image from the host named by the catalog entry. That host receives the image URL and ordinary network information, including your IP address. Hosts may include a product brand, Open Beauty Facts, or a content-delivery provider, and can change as catalog entries change. Image requests do not include your routine or skin profile.

Product catalog information and images may be incomplete, outdated, or supplied by third parties. Review product and ingredient information before relying on it.

6. Brand, retailer, and affiliate links

The app may provide a link to a brand or retailer. When you choose to open a link, the destination receives the request and any product or campaign identifier included in the link, along with ordinary network information. The app does not intentionally add your routine or skin profile to the destination URL. The destination’s privacy policy applies after you leave the app.

Some product suggestions may use an affiliate link. When the app identifies a link as an affiliate link, it labels the link and explains that Amanchi Labs may earn a commission. Any commission does not determine the product’s suitability or position in results. Link availability and type can vary with the catalog and app configuration.

7. Purchases

The app may offer a one-time Pro Offline purchase. Apple processes App Store purchases and restores and may provide the app with transaction or entitlement information needed to unlock or restore the purchase. DermaPrivate does not receive your payment-card number. Apple’s privacy terms apply to its handling of purchase information. Clearing app data does not remove a purchase record held by Apple; use the app’s restore-purchase control with the relevant Apple account.

8. How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide the website and app features you request, keep your routine available on your device, perform an online product search or catalog refresh you choose or the app initiates, provide an AI response after authorization, enable or restore an in-app purchase, respond to support, and maintain and secure these services.

Information is sent to another service only for the feature or action described in this policy: website hosting and delivery providers; the DermaPrivate catalog service; Open Beauty Facts for an online product lookup; Groq for an authorized AI request; Apple for a purchase or restore; and an image, brand, or retailer host contacted by a request you initiate or by a displayed remote image. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information for money or use routine information for targeted advertising.

The app does not send routine information to a DermaPrivate account server and does not provide account-based routine synchronization in the current release. This does not limit the independent data practices of Apple, Groq, Open Beauty Facts, website or image hosts, or brand and retailer sites.

9. Retention and deletion

Routine, profile, and preference information stays in local app storage until you clear the app’s data, remove the app or its storage, or overwrite the information. Settings provides a Clear all local app data control and a separate control to clear local AI activity history. You can remove your Groq key and revoke AI authorization separately. These controls do not erase data already sent to a provider, information included in a device backup, or an exported backup you control.

DermaPrivate does not keep a server copy of your routine to retrieve or delete. Apple, Groq, Open Beauty Facts, website and catalog hosts, image hosts, and retailers control the information they receive and their own retention and deletion practices. We do not promise a retention period for their request logs. For information held by one of these providers, use that provider’s privacy or account controls.

Support emails are retained in the support mailbox for as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable support and business records. The email provider may retain copies under its own settings and practices.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You can use core routine features without AI or an online product search. You can choose whether to authorize AI, ask before each AI request, remove your key, clear AI activity history, clear local app data, use device backups, or open a product link.

For privacy questions or requests about information we control, contact support@amanchilabs.com. Please do not email sensitive skincare or health information unless it is necessary to make your request. We may ask for information to verify a request. Because the app has no account or routine-sync service, we generally cannot access or remotely erase information stored only on your device. Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete, copy, or restrict certain personal information. You may also contact your local privacy regulator.

11. Security

The app uses device local storage for routine data and the operating system’s secure credential storage for the Groq key. App network integrations use HTTPS endpoints. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device with a passcode, keep its operating system current, and consider your device backup settings. The security practices of third-party providers are governed by their own policies and controls.

12. Children

DermaPrivate is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website or app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact support@amanchilabs.com so we can review the request. App information stored only on a device is not available for us to identify or remove remotely.

13. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Groq describes its limited reliability or abuse-related retention as occurring in Google Cloud locations in the United States. Other providers’ locations and transfer practices are described in their own current privacy notices. We do not control where those providers process information.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, website, providers, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be available at www.dermaprivate.app/privacy/ and in the app’s Settings. The effective and updated dates at the top identify the version. If a change materially affects an optional app feature, we will provide an appropriate in-app notice or authorization update before using that feature where required.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Amanchi Labs at support@amanchilabs.com.
