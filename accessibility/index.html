Accessibility

We want DermaPrivate to be usable by more people. The mobile app accessibility evaluation is still in progress.

Review status: evaluation pending. We are not making claims here about support for VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, or other accessibility features until representative app tasks have been evaluated.

What has and has not been checked

Some focused large-text checks have been run, but that is not a complete app review. VoiceOver has not been evaluated across representative tasks. Broader checks of reading order, contrast, touch targets, and key app flows are still needed.

We do not yet have a complete, tested list of app accessibility features or limitations to publish. Please contact us if you encounter a barrier or need information before using the app.

Share accessibility feedback

Email support@amanchilabs.com. If you can, include the app screen or task where you encountered the issue and the assistive setting you were using. Please do not include sensitive skincare or health information.
